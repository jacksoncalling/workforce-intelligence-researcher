# The Five Ds Framework

The DAA FuE department uses five megatrend lenses to frame their research and publications. These are not intake categories — incoming signals get coarse-sorted first (see identity.md, Stage 1). The five Ds are applied when moving from sorting to synthesis: writing pattern reports, briefings, and publications.

Not every finding maps to a single D. Tag with multiple Ds when a finding sits at an intersection. Tag as **cross-cutting** or **emergent** when a finding doesn't fit cleanly — the Ds are a communication framework, not a filter that discards what doesn't belong.

---

## The Five Ds

### 1. Decarbonization (Dekarbonisierung)

The transition to a climate-neutral economy creates, transforms, and eliminates job profiles.

**What to watch for in signals:**
- New job titles or certifications related to sustainability
- Coachees from fossil-fuel-adjacent industries seeking transition
- Green skills appearing in job descriptions for traditionally non-green roles
- Training gaps: workers want to transition but lack recognized qualifications
- Regional differences: Ruhrgebiet vs. rural NRW vs. Rhineland

**Key external sources:** UBA (Umweltbundesamt), BIBB Ausbildungsberufe updates, EU Green Deal workforce reports

### 2. Digitalization (Digitalisierung)

AI, automation, platform work, and digital skill requirements reshape every sector.

**What to watch for in signals:**
- AI tool proficiency appearing as interview requirement
- Positions disappearing vs. positions transforming (different problems)
- Platform work / gig economy as alternative to traditional employment
- Digital divide: who gets left behind, by age, by sector, by qualification
- Coachees' own AI literacy and its effect on job search effectiveness

**Key external sources:** IAB Forschungsberichte, BMAS Arbeit-4.0, Bitkom Arbeitsmarkt reports

### 3. Demographic Change (Demografischer Wandel)

Aging workforce, migration, skills shortage, and generational shifts in work expectations.

**What to watch for in signals:**
- Fachkraeftemangel: where is it real vs. where is it reported but not experienced?
- International coachees: recognition delays, language barriers, hidden qualifications
- Age discrimination: what coaches observe vs. what statistics show
- Regional workforce migration patterns within Germany
- Care responsibilities affecting career re-entry (gendered, often invisible)

**Key external sources:** BA Fachkraefteengpassanalyse, BAMF Integrationskurse data, Destatis Bevoelkerungsvorausberechnung

### 4. Democracy (Demokratie)

Civic participation, institutional trust, polarization, and their effects on work culture and labor markets.

**What to watch for in signals:**
- Coachees' trust in institutions (Arbeitsagentur, Bildungstraeger, employers)
- Political climate affecting hiring (e.g., attitudes toward international candidates)
- Workplace culture signals: respect, autonomy, psychological safety
- Public sector vs. private sector working conditions and attractiveness

**Key external sources:** Bertelsmann Stiftung social cohesion studies, FES Arbeitspapiere, WSI Reports

### 5. Decent Work (Gute Arbeit)

Fair wages, working conditions, respect, and dignity in employment.

**What to watch for in signals:**
- Coachees accepting positions below their qualification level — why?
- Working conditions as reason for leaving positions (not just finding them)
- Salary transparency: what coachees report vs. what is posted
- Befristung (temporary contracts) patterns across sectors
- Respect and recognition in service professions (Pflege, Handel, Gastronomie)

**Key external sources:** DGB Gute-Arbeit-Index, WSI Tarifarchiv, IAQ Duisburg working conditions research

## Intersection Analysis

The most interesting research questions sit where two or more Ds overlap:

| Intersection | Example Question |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Digitalization + Decent Work | Does AI automation improve or worsen working conditions in the jobs that remain? |
| Decarbonization + Demographic Change | Can the green transition absorb workers displaced from traditional industries, or does it require skills they don't have? |
| Digitalization + Demographic Change | Are older workers being excluded from digital transformation, or are they adapting differently than expected? |
| Democracy + Decent Work | Does declining institutional trust affect coachees' willingness to engage with public employment services? |
| Decarbonization + Decent Work | Are "green jobs" actually good jobs, or are they repeating the precarity patterns of the gig economy? |

When tagging signals and research, always check: does this touch a second D? If so, the finding is likely more significant than it first appears.
