# Monthly Policy Scan

**Schedule:** 15th of each month
**Output:** `research-feed/monthly/YYYY-MM_policy-scan.md`

---

## Prompt

Scan the following policy sources for new developments since the last scan:

- **BMAS** (bmas.de) — new legislation, Buergergeld changes, Fachkraefteeinwanderung updates
- **EU Employment Policy** (ec.europa.eu/social) — European Pillar of Social Rights developments
- **EUR-Lex** (eur-lex.europa.eu) — AI Act workforce provisions, Green Deal employment measures
- **Bundesgesetzblatt** (bgbl.de) — new laws affecting labor market, education, migration

For each relevant development:

1. Summarize in 2-3 sentences
2. Tag with the relevant D(s) from the five-Ds framework
3. Assess practical impact: what does this mean for coaching programs and the people in them?
4. Rate urgency: informational (good to know), operational (affects current programs), or strategic (requires program redesign)

If a development is rated operational or strategic, draft a short briefing (max 1 page) for the FuE team.

Check against coach signals: are coaches already seeing effects of recent policy changes on the ground? If yes, note the convergence.

Write the output to `research-feed/monthly/` with the month in the filename.
