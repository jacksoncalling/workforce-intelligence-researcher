# Quarterly Full Compound

**Schedule:** First week of each quarter (January, April, July, October)
**Output:** `research-feed/quarterly/YYYY-QX_full-compound.md`

---

## Prompt

This is the most comprehensive research routine. Review everything the system has produced in the past quarter.

**Step 1 — Gather all inputs:**
- All monthly signal compound reports from this quarter
- All weekly IAB and BA scan results
- All monthly policy scan results
- Any ad-hoc research briefings produced this quarter
- Any anomaly deep-dives completed

**Step 2 — Synthesize across the quarter:**

For each of the five Ds (Decarbonization, Digitalization, Demographic Change, Democracy, Decent Work):
- What did we learn this quarter?
- Where did human sensing and external research converge?
- Where did they contradict? What does the contradiction tell us?
- What questions were answered?
- What new questions emerged?

**Step 3 — Assess the sensing network:**
- Which regions, industries, or demographics contributed the most signals?
- Where are we blind? Which Ds are underrepresented?
- Are any coach teams contributing more than others? (Note the gap, not the identity.)
- Were the sensing prompts we issued this quarter useful? Did they generate signals?

**Step 4 — Update the framework:**
- Does `reference/five-ds-framework.md` need updating based on new patterns?
- Are there intersection patterns (two Ds overlapping) we should add?
- Should any signal types be added to the taxonomy?

**Step 5 — Produce outputs:**
1. Quarterly intelligence briefing for FuE leadership (max 3 pages)
2. Updated sensing priorities for the next quarter
3. 3-5 new sensing prompts for the coaching network
4. List of topics worth a deeper research investigation

Write all outputs to `research-feed/quarterly/`.
