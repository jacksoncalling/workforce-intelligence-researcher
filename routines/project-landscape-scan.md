# Project Landscape Scan

**Trigger:** FuE team starts a new research project
**Output:** `research-feed/project-briefs/YYYY-MM-DD_[project-name].md`
**Turnaround:** Within 24 hours

---

## Prompt

The FuE team is starting a new project on: [TOPIC — fill in before running]

Before any new research begins, audit what we already know.

**Step 1 — Internal intelligence:**
- Search `shared-intelligence/signals/` for any signals related to this topic
- Search `shared-intelligence/patterns/` for relevant pattern reports
- Check previous research outputs for any briefings that touched this area
- How many signals do we have? From how many coaches? How recent?

**Step 2 — External baseline:**
- What do the key sources (IAB, BA, BIBB, BMAS) currently say about this topic?
- Are there recent academic publications (last 12 months)?
- Is there EU-level policy activity on this?

**Step 3 — Gap analysis:**
- Where is our sensing strong? Where is it weak?
- Which of the five Ds does this project touch? Are those Ds well-covered in our signal base?
- What regions or industries should we be hearing from but aren't?

**Step 4 — Produce initial briefing:**
- Summary of what we already know (with confidence levels)
- What we don't know and need to investigate
- 3-5 sensing prompts to send to coaches immediately
- Suggested external research agenda for the first month

Write the briefing to `research-feed/project-briefs/` with the project name in the filename.
