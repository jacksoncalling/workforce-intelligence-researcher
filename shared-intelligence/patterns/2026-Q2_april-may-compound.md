## Confirmed Patterns

### IT Hiring Funnel Breaking at Offer Stage
- **Confidence:** Medium → trending toward High
- **Signals:** 3 coaches, 7+ coachees
- **Lenses:** Digitalization
- **Status:** Contradicts BA vacancy data. The statistical picture shows growth; the human sensing shows paralysis. Hypothesis: budget freezes and AI-uncertainty causing interview-without-intent.
- **Action taken:** Sensing prompt issued (see sensing-prompts/2026-05_it-offer-stage.md)
- **Research cross-reference:**
  - BA Arbeitsmarktbericht (April 2026): IT vacancies in NRW up 8% year-over-year. This is the contradiction — more postings, but coaches report fewer offers.
  - Bitkom Arbeitsmarkt Q1 2026: 42% of IT companies report "difficulty filling positions" — but the report does not distinguish between "cannot find candidates" and "not authorized to hire." Our coaches suggest the latter.
  - IAB Stellenerhebung (Q4 2025): Average vacancy duration in IT increased from 156 to 183 days nationally. Consistent with the stall pattern — positions stay open longer because the funnel breaks at the offer stage, not at the application stage.

### Nursing Positions: Statistical Improvement Masks Ground-Level Stagnation
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Signals:** 2 coaches, 5 coachees
- **Lenses:** Decent Work + Demographic Change
- **Status:** Ghost postings + rapid turnover + recognition delays create an invisible shortage. IAB data cannot see this.
- **Next step:** Need signals from coaches in other healthcare sub-sectors (Altenpflege, ambulante Pflege) to determine if this is hospital-specific or sector-wide.
- **Research cross-reference:**
  - IAB Kurzbericht 8/2026: Reports 12% decrease in unfilled nursing positions in NRW. Our coaches see the opposite — the positions are disappearing from postings, not being filled.
  - BA Fachkraefteengpassanalyse (Q1 2026): Nursing still listed as top-10 shortage profession. But the methodology counts postings vs. qualified unemployed — it cannot see ghost postings or rapid turnover.
  - BAMF Anerkennungsstatistik 2025: Average recognition time for international nursing qualifications: 8.4 months. Matches what our coaches report (6-12 months). During this time, the position is "unfilled" in statistics but "not actually available" in reality.
  - DGB Gute-Arbeit-Index 2025: Nursing scores lowest in "work-life balance" and "physical demands" across all professions. Consistent with the rapid turnover coaches observe — the positions exist, but the conditions drive people out.

## What the System Learned This Month

The most important finding is not any single pattern — it is that our sensing network has significant blind spots in sectors where the Fachkraeftemangel narrative is strongest (nursing, trades, logistics). We are sensing well in IT and administration (because our coaches have coachees there) but have no visibility into exactly the sectors where policy claims the biggest shortages exist. This means we cannot independently verify or challenge the dominant narrative with lived experience data.

**Priority for next month:** Fill the sector blind spots before deepening existing patterns.
