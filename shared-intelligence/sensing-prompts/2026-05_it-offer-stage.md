# Sensing Prompt: IT Offer-Stage Stalls

**From:** FuE Research Team
**Date:** 2026-05-15
**Expires:** 2026-06-30 (review after 6 weeks)
**Priority:** Medium — active investigation

---

## What we are investigating

Multiple coaches report IT-sector coachees experiencing interviews that proceed normally through round 1-2 but then go silent — no rejection, no offer, no communication. We want to understand whether this is:

- A: Budget freezes (companies interviewing but not authorized to hire)
- B: AI-uncertainty (hiring managers delaying decisions until automation strategy is clear)
- C: Compliance posting (positions posted to satisfy internal policy, not actual openings)
- D: Something else entirely

## What to ask your IT coachees

In your next sessions with IT-sector coachees (developers, consultants, project managers, admins), ask:

1. **Timeline question:** "How quickly did you get your first interview after applying? And how long has it been since your last interview step?" (We are looking for fast-start-then-stall patterns.)

2. **Communication question:** "After the interview went quiet, did you follow up? What happened?" (We want to know if companies respond at all, and what they say.)

3. **Company size question:** "Was this a startup, Mittelstand, or enterprise?" (We suspect the pattern is concentrated in mid-size companies.)

4. **Comparison question:** "Is this different from how hiring worked when you got your last job?" (Establishes whether the coachee perceives a change.)

## How to report back

After you have 2-3 data points, write a signal entry to `shared-intelligence/signals/` using the standard format. Tag it with:
- **Type:** hiring-behavior
- **Lens:** Digitalization
- **Reference this prompt:** "Response to sensing-prompt 2026-05_it-offer-stage"

You do not need to wait until you have a strong pattern. Even "my 2 IT coachees this month did NOT experience this" is valuable data — it helps us understand where the pattern holds and where it doesn't.

## What we already know

- 3 coaches in Aachen, Koeln, Duesseldorf have reported this pattern
- 7+ coachees confirm fast-interview-then-silence in the last 3 months
- BA data shows IT vacancies INCREASING in NRW — which contradicts what coaches observe
- The contradiction is the interesting finding. Help us understand it.
