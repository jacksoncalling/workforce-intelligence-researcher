## Signal: Nursing positions posted but not actively hiring — ghost postings

**Date:** 2026-04-28
**Region:** NRW
**Industry:** Pflege / Healthcare
**Type:** hiring-behavior
**Lens:** Decent Work + Demographic Change
**Confidence:** Medium (2 coaches, 5 coachees)

### Observation

Coachees with nursing qualifications report applying to posted positions and receiving no response — or receiving responses that reveal the position is "not currently being filled." Two coachees accepted positions that turned out to have significantly different conditions than posted (shift patterns, patient ratios). Both left within 8 weeks. One coach notes that international candidates with recognized qualifications face 6-12 month administrative delays during which the "open position" remains statistically unfilled.

### Context

IAB Kurzbericht (March 2026) reports 12% decrease in unfilled nursing positions in NRW — suggesting the Fachkraeftemangel is easing. Coach observations suggest the opposite: employers may have stopped posting positions they cannot fill under current working conditions, or are posting for compliance rather than actual hiring. The statistical improvement may mask workforce resignation.

### Coaching Implication

Coaches working with nursing-qualified coachees should verify position status before encouraging applications (call the Pflegedienstleitung directly). For international candidates: factor recognition timelines into career planning — 6-12 months is not a gap, it is a phase that needs its own strategy. Flag working conditions explicitly — a nursing position that looks like a win on paper may be a trap if the conditions cause rapid turnover.
