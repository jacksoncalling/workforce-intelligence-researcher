## Signal: IT-sector interviews going silent after second round

**Date:** 2026-04-12
**Region:** NRW (Aachen, Koeln, Duesseldorf)
**Industry:** IT / Software Development
**Type:** hiring-behavior
**Lens:** Digitalization
**Confidence:** Medium (3 coaches, 7 coachees reporting similar pattern)

### Observation

Multiple IT-sector coachees report a consistent pattern: first-round interviews proceed normally (often within days of application), but after the second round, communication stops entirely. No rejection, no timeline, no response to follow-up emails. This pattern has appeared across mid-size companies (50-500 employees) and is new — same coachees report normal hiring processes 6-12 months ago.

### Context

This contradicts BA vacancy data showing IT positions are increasing in NRW. If interviews are happening but offers are not, the vacancy statistics may be measuring postings rather than actual hiring intent. Possible explanations: budget freezes after Q1 results, AI-uncertainty causing hiring committees to delay, or compliance-driven posting (positions posted to meet internal policy but not actually funded).

### Coaching Implication

Coaches should prepare IT coachees for longer timelines and help them maintain momentum despite silence. Consider adding a "what to do when they go quiet" module to interview prep. Also: track which company sizes and sub-sectors show this pattern — if it is concentrated in certain areas, coaches can set expectations earlier.
